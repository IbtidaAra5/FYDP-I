# MASTER PROJECT CONTEXT DOCUMENT

## 1. Project Overview & Title

* **Working Title:** Trust Calibration in Legal AI: Evaluating Interface Design and User Expertise in Detecting LLM Hallucinations for Bangladeshi Statutory Law.
* **Project Domain:** Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Explainable AI (XAI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Legal Tech.
* **Project Duration:** 1 Academic Year (Final Year Design Project - FYDP).
* **Context:** Designed to fulfill the Institutional of Engineers, Bangladesh (BAETE) criteria for Complex Engineering Problems (P1–P7) and Complex Engineering Activities (A1–A5).

## 2. Problem Statement

While Large Language Models (LLMs) can democratize access to legal information, they frequently **hallucinate** facts—fabricating clauses, misinterpreting sections, or blending outdated laws. This risk is heavily magnified in low-resource languages like Bangla.

Rather than chasing an impossible algorithmic guarantee of 100% accuracy, this project addresses the socio-technical human element: **How can interface design empower users with varying levels of expertise to accurately detect, verify, and calibrated their trust against AI legal hallucinations?**

---

## 3. Scope of Work: The AI/ML Backend (Pure RAG Architecture)

To ensure engineering feasibility within a 1-year timeline, the project bypasses the high-compute risk of full model fine-tuning. Instead, it leverages a robust **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** framework.

* **Primary Corpus:** The **Bangladesh Road Transport Act, 2018** acts as the initial statutory dataset, with design provisions to scale to other legal sectors.
* **Dataset Generation:** Construction of an expert-validated, native Bangla QA dataset consisting of legal scenarios, complex queries, and specific edge cases mapped to official legal clauses.
* **Retrieval Pipeline:** Utilizing state-of-the-art, open-source multilingual or Bangla-specific embedding models to vectorise the legal documents.
* **Generation Pipeline:** Interfacing via API or local hosting with powerful open-weight LLMs (e.g., Llama, Qwen, Gemma) constrained via prompt engineering and RAG injection to synthesize answers strictly from the fetched legal text.

---

## 4. Scope of Work: The HCI & UI/UX Frontend

The frontend is a web application designed to isolate and test how different visual treatments affect a human's ability to catch system hallucinations. It features three distinct interface setups:

* **Treatment A (The Baseline Interface):** A standard, plain conversational chatbot interface (similar to standard ChatGPT) with text-only outputs, no source tracking, and no error warnings.
* **Treatment B (The Traceability Interface):** Introduces interactive inline citations and tooltips. Users can hover over or click any statement generated by the AI to instantly view the exact source paragraph, section, and page from the official Government Gazette.
* **Treatment C (The XAI / Uncertainty Visualization Interface):** Integrates Explainable AI elements, including visual confidence bars, token-level highlighting (color-coding words where the backend retrieval confidence is low), and explicit modal warnings when system uncertainty crosses a threshold.

---

## 5. User Testing & Evaluation Framework (Trust Calibration Study)

The core research objective is to analyze the intersection of **Interface Design** and **User Domain Knowledge**. The user study utilizes a $3 \times 2$ matrix, testing the 3 UI treatments across 2 distinct user demographics:

### Target User Demographics:

1. **Laymen:** Everyday Bangladeshi citizens with no formal legal background.
2. **Legal Experts:** Law students, legal researchers, or practicing lawyers with domain expertise.

### Empirical Evaluation Metrics:

* **Hallucination Detection Rate:** The percentage of pre-programmed or organic AI errors successfully identified and rejected by the user.
* **Trust Calibration Score:** Measuring over-reliance (blindly trusting a wrong answer) versus under-reliance (dismissing a correct answer).
* **Cognitive Load:** Quantified using the standardized **NASA-TLX (Task Load Index)** framework to ensure advanced UI elements do not induce mental fatigue.
* **System Usability:** Measured using the standardized **System Usability Scale (SUS)**.
